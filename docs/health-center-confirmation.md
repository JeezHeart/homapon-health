## About this form (please read first)

Your health center is adopting a digital system for child immunization
records. Before it handles real records, we must confirm that the
vaccine schedules, record fields, and working rules inside the system
match your center's current official requirements. **You are the
authority** — this form only records your answers.

**How to accomplish this form (about 15–20 minutes):**

1. Use a pen. For each item, **encircle** your answer.
2. **Yes** = what is shown matches your center's current requirement.
3. **No** = it does not — please write the correction in the space
   provided. Corrections are the most valuable answers on this form.
4. There are no wrong answers. If a rule varies in practice, say so.
5. Parts I–IV need your answers. Part V is the sign-off.

This form collects professional opinions on recordkeeping only. It is
not a medical examination and gives no clinical advice.
